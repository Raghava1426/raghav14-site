Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Daily Little Wins ("we") runs raghav14.in and sells digital products (ebooks, printable planners and templates) through Gumroad and Payhip. This policy explains what information we handle.

Purchases

Payments and downloads are processed by Gumroad (gumroad.com) and Payhip (payhip.com). We receive the information those platforms share with sellers, such as your name, email address and what you bought, so we can deliver your files and answer support questions. We never see or store your card details.

Website

raghav14.in is a static site. We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal data.

Pinterest integration

We use the Pinterest API only to publish Pins to our own Pinterest account and to read our own boards. We do not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users.

Emails

If you join our email list, we use your email only to send updates about our products. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete information we hold about you, email raghavakrish14@raghav14.in.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
